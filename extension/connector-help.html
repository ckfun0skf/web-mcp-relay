Connect your AI agent

Use an AI client that supports custom remote MCP connectors.

  1. Connect this site

    Click Connect Now and wait for Connected.

  2. Add the connector

    In your AI client's connector settings:

    MCP URL: the session panel's URL ending in /mcp

    Authentication: None (this MVP)

  3. Copy & paste

    Click Copy Session & Connector Details in the session panel. Paste into your trusted AI conversation.

Keep the session key private. Never share OWNER_SECRET.

Tool calls & approvals

Use list_browser_tools with session_key, then call_browser_tool with the returned tool_id, snapshot and arguments.

Approve requests in the extension popup. Session trust is optional; client/browser approvals remain independent. Authentication None does not remove the session-key requirement.

Need help?
  • No tools: the site must expose WebMCP tools.
  • Disconnected: resume through the extension popup.
  • Expired session: reconnect and copy the new key.
  • Page refreshed: list tools again; trust resets.
  • Write outcome unknown: check before retrying.
  • No custom remote MCP support: use a compatible client. Copying session details alone does not add a connector.
